Runs in your office, not the cloud

Zero-cloud HIPAA architecture.

  • Installs on your practice's own computer. Patient data is read, processed, and stored locally — it never leaves your office network.
  • No cloud ingestion of patient data, and no new breach surface to worry about.
  • The engine is robotic process automation — software doing exactly what a trained front-desk employee does: reading the schedule, logging into portals, writing down what it finds.
  • No generative AI. No ChatGPT. No patient data sent to any AI service — decisions are made by fixed, deterministic rules.

About the Gmail sign-in

Some carrier portals email a one-time code at login. If your office connects a Google account, InstantVerify uses secure, read-only Google sign-in to retrieve just that code and complete the login. Your email, credentials, and patient data never leave your building. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail.
